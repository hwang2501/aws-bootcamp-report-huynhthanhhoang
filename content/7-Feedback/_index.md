During my internship at the **First Cloud Journey (FCJ)** program, I gained valuable technical knowledge and practical experience in AWS Cloud. Below are my personal evaluations and suggestions after completing the internship.

---

## General Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

The working environment was professional, friendly, and supportive. Team members were always willing to answer questions and share their experience. The learning materials and lab instructions were well organized, making it easier to follow the internship roadmap.

### 2. Mentor and Admin Support

The mentor provided clear technical guidance and encouraged independent problem-solving instead of simply giving answers. The admin team was responsive and helped interns quickly resolve administrative issues whenever needed.

### 3. Relevance to My Major

The internship matched my Software Engineering major very well. Through AWS services such as EC2, VPC, IAM, S3, Lambda, and Bedrock, I was able to apply theoretical knowledge to practical cloud solutions.

### 4. Learning Opportunities

This internship significantly improved both my technical and soft skills. Besides learning AWS services, I also developed documentation, presentation, teamwork, and self-learning skills that will benefit my future career.

### 5. Team Culture

The FCJ community maintained a positive, collaborative, and respectful culture. Everyone was willing to share knowledge, making it easy for interns to learn from experienced members.

### 6. Internship Benefits

Although the internship was unpaid, it provided many valuable opportunities, including access to AWS Credits, hands-on labs, technical workshops, community events, and real-world cloud projects.

---

## Questions

### What are you most satisfied with during your internship?

I am most satisfied with the opportunity to learn AWS through practical labs and projects instead of only studying theory. Working with real cloud services helped me understand how AWS is used in real business environments.

### What do you think could be improved?

I suggest providing a more detailed onboarding guide during the first week, including the reporting format, project structure, and recommended learning path. This would help new interns adapt more quickly.

### Would you recommend this internship to your friends?

Yes. I would highly recommend FCJ to students who are interested in Cloud Computing, AWS, DevOps, or AI. It provides a structured learning roadmap, practical experience, and a supportive community.

---

## Suggestions

### Recommendations

- Organize more technical sharing sessions between interns.
- Hold additional hands-on workshops focusing on real AWS architectures.
- Increase opportunities for group projects to improve collaboration skills.
- Continue organizing community events that connect students with industry professionals.

### Future Expectations

I hope to continue learning with the FCJ community and have the opportunity to participate in future cloud projects or advanced AWS training programs.

### Final Thoughts

I sincerely appreciate the support from the FCJ organizers, mentors, and community members throughout this internship. This experience has strengthened my cloud knowledge, improved my practical skills, and motivated me to continue pursuing a career in Cloud Computing and AWS.
